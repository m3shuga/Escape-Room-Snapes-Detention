# Snape's Detention: A Digital Escape Room for the English Classroom

**Play it here:** https://m3shuga.github.io/Escape-Room-Snapes-Detention/

Snape’s Detention is a free digital escape room inspired by the world of Harry Potter, made for English teachers who want their students to learn through play. Students team up to escape Professor Snape’s detention by solving a series of English challenges: reading, vocabulary, grammar, riddles and more, all against the clock. With three difficulty levels, built-in hints and a class-wide House Cup, it turns a regular English lesson into an adventure. No accounts, no installation and no preparation needed. Just open the link and let the mischief begin.

It was made for English lessons in lower secondary school (ages 13–16, grades 8–10), but works for any learners at roughly A2–B2 level.

## What students practise

The challenges are built around everyday English-classroom skills:

- reading comprehension and following instructions
- vocabulary and word knowledge
- grammar (verb tenses, irregular verbs, commonly confused words and more)
- proofreading
- riddles and wordplay
- idioms and expressions
- (Hard difficulty only) Checking students' knowledge of Harry Potter lore. 

## Difficulty levels

Teams choose a level at the start:

- **Easy:** simpler English. No knowledge of the Harry Potter books or films is needed. Most hints are free.
- **Medium:** more demanding English. Hints cost house points.
- **Hard:** advanced English, plus questions that reward real Harry Potter knowledge. Hints cost more house points.

This makes it easy to use the same escape room with mixed-ability groups or across different grades.

## How it works

1. Each team opens the link (or the downloaded file) on its own computer.
2. The adventure begins with a mysterious blank page. Students must work out for themselves how to get started.
3. Teams enter a team name, choose a Hogwarts house and pick a difficulty level.
4. A 45-minute countdown starts, and the escape begins.
5. Teams earn **house points** as they go. Hints are available if they get stuck, but they may come at a price.
6. **Think before you answer!** Careless guessing has consequences in Snape's castle.
7. The adventure ends when the team either escapes or runs out of time.

Progress is saved automatically on each computer, so a refreshed page or a closed browser tab does not lose a team's progress.

### Music and sound

The game has optional atmospheric music and sound effects. Music starts switched off. Headphones are recommended if many teams play in the same room.

## The House Cup (class results)

When a team finishes, their screen shows a short **report code**. The teacher collects these codes on their own computer and can show a **House Cup** results board on the projector, with house point totals and a ranking of all teams. This works without any internet connection between the computers.

## For teachers

The escape room includes a **hidden teacher panel**. From it you can see the answers, help a team that is stuck, adjust the time, manage the House Cup results and reset a computer for the next class.

To keep it secret from students, the teacher panel and how to open it are **not described here**. If you are a teacher and would like access, contact the creator, @m3shuga.

### Tips for the lesson

- Test the link on the school network before the lesson.
- One computer per team works best. Teams of 2–3 students give good discussion.
- Encourage teams to discuss answers in English before they submit them.
- Plan a few minutes at the end to show the House Cup and talk about the language the students used.

## Running it yourself

The whole escape room is a single file, `index.html`. You can:

- **use the link** at the top of this page, or
- **download `index.html`** and open it in any browser. It works offline, though the fonts look best with an internet connection.

## Privacy

Everything is stored only in the browser on each computer, including team names, progress and the teacher's results board. No information is sent to the internet, to GitHub or to anyone else. NOTE: Students should still use team names rather than their full names.

To remove all saved information from a computer, delete the website's data in the browser settings, or use the reset option in the teacher panel.
